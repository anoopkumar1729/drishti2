Workflow

Three steps, one read.

Drishti slots into the workflow clinicians already use — it doesn't replace the radiologist, it clears the queue faster and flags what needs eyes first.

01 / INGEST

Pull the full picture

Imaging (X-ray, CT, MRI), lab results, and EHR history sync in automatically from existing hospital systems — no separate upload step.

→
02 / ANALYZE

Cross-reference, not just scan

The model reads imaging alongside labs and history, so a borderline finding gets weighed against the patient's actual clinical context.

→
03 / REPORT

A flagged, explainable report

Clinicians get an annotated report with confidence scores and the reasoning behind each flag — ready to confirm, adjust, or dismiss.
